The printed page sets all of this inside the oval, and from 1280px up it still fits there. Narrower it does not: the wreath's clear interior is ~65% of its width, so copy that fits inside it is either unreadably narrow or forces the artwork so far past the viewport that both floral flanks clip off-screen entirely and the oval stops reading as an oval. Below `xl` the oval therefore frames the names lockup alone and this block follows it in clear space. The breakpoint is `xl`, not `md` — see the arithmetic in wildflower_wreath.html. At `md` the frame is still ~360px short of the height this copy needs, which put its top line 87% under painted flowers on every tablet and small laptop. Duplicating the markup rather than reordering keeps one flat DOM per breakpoint; the copy that isn't used is `display:none`, so assistive technology never sees both.
